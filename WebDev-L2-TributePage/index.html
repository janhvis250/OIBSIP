THE STORY

More Than An Entrepreneur

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur, inventor and designer whose work helped shape the modern technology industry.

Born in 1955 and raised in California, Jobs developed an early interest in electronics and design. His curiosity eventually led him to technology and entrepreneurship.

In 1976, he co-founded Apple with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne. The company began by creating personal computers and later introduced products that changed the way people interacted with technology.

After leaving Apple in 1985, Jobs founded NeXT and became involved with Pixar. His experience outside Apple broadened his approach to technology, creativity and product design.

Jobs returned to Apple in 1997. During the years that followed, Apple introduced products such as the iMac, iPod, iPhone and iPad, combining technology with an emphasis on simplicity and design.

THE JOURNEY

Moments That Defined a Career

1955

The Beginning

Steve Jobs was born in San Francisco, California, and was raised in the surrounding area.

1976

Apple Is Founded

Jobs and Steve Wozniak co-founded Apple, beginning a company that would become one of the world's most influential technology companies.

1984

Macintosh

Apple introduced the Macintosh, bringing graphical computing and a mouse-driven interface to a broader audience.

1985

A New Chapter

Jobs left Apple and later founded NeXT, beginning another chapter in his career in computer technology.

1986

Pixar

Jobs acquired the computer graphics division that became Pixar, helping build a company that transformed computer animation.

1997

Return to Apple

Jobs returned to Apple after the company acquired NeXT and became central to its next phase of product development.

2007

The iPhone

Apple introduced the iPhone, bringing together a phone, an internet communicator and an iPod in one device.

2011

His Final Chapter

Jobs resigned as Apple's CEO in August 2011 and became chairman. He passed away on October 5, 2011.

THE LEGACY

Think.
Create.
Change.

Steve Jobs' influence extended beyond individual products. His approach brought technology, design and user experience closer together.

His career is often remembered for emphasizing simplicity, attention to detail and the idea that technology should be accessible and meaningful to the people using it.

From personal computers to smartphones and digital media, the products associated with his career became part of everyday life for millions of people.
